# OKG Manager / Zeppelin — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the OKG team (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use OKG Manager / Zeppelin (the “Bot”), its dashboard, and related services.

## 1. Information the service may process
Depending on enabled features and server configuration, the service may process:

- **Discord identifiers and server details:** user IDs, server IDs, server names, icons, server-owner IDs, channel and role IDs, and information needed for dashboard access.
- **Configuration and access records:** server configuration, dashboard permission assignments, configuration revisions, access changes, and security/audit events.
- **Moderation and support records:** moderation cases and actions, notes, ticket messages, ticket transcripts, and information server staff submit.
- **Command and operational data:** command inputs needed to perform an action, error reports, and technical logs used to operate, diagnose, and secure the service.
- **Messages and content:** content supplied to commands, submitted to tickets, or processed by enabled moderation/logging features. The Bot receives Discord events required for enabled functionality; it is not intended to collect every private conversation.

The exact data processed depends on the features and configuration used by each server.

## 2. How information is used
We use information to operate the Bot and dashboard; enforce server authorization and access permissions; provide moderation, logging, support, and configuration features; diagnose errors; prevent abuse; maintain security and auditability; and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Platform-owner access
Discord user ID **1441223435043868735** is the designated OKG platform owner and is intended to have full dashboard access to every server authorized to use the Bot, including server configuration and dashboard-access settings. This account is identified as **Platform owner** and cannot be removed or downgraded by a server dashboard manager. Server owners should consider this access before inviting the Bot. Do not use the service if your server does not accept this arrangement.

## 4. Sharing and service providers
We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting, database, monitoring, or infrastructure providers used to run the Bot. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or address a security incident.

## 5. Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain moderation history and audit records, troubleshoot issues, enforce our terms, and meet legal or security needs. Retention varies by feature and server configuration. Removing the Bot does not necessarily immediately delete stored configuration, moderation records, tickets, backups, or audit logs. Contact us about a specific deletion request; some records may need to be retained for legitimate reasons.

## 6. Security
We use reasonable operational and technical measures intended to protect stored information, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep Discord credentials, bot tokens, and API keys private. Report suspected exposure through the support server.

## 7. Your choices and requests
Server owners can remove the Bot and stop using the dashboard. For questions or requests about information handled by the Bot, contact us through the [OKG support server](https://discord.gg/2nHAng3gnr) and include enough detail to identify the server or record without sharing passwords or tokens. We will review requests as appropriate under applicable law.

## 8. Children and Discord
The service is intended for people permitted to use Discord under Discord’s applicable terms and age requirements. We do not knowingly seek information from children who are not permitted to use Discord.

## 9. International processing
The service and its providers may process or store information in countries other than where you live. Data-protection rights may vary by location.

## 10. Changes
We may update this Policy as the service changes. Material revisions will be reflected in the effective date above.

## 11. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, use the [OKG support server](https://discord.gg/2nHAng3gnr). Do not include passwords, bot tokens, or API keys.
